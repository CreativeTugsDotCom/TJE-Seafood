TJE Seafood is proudly run by the Cochran family. We actively fish out of the Gulf of Mexico to bring our customers the absolute freshest seafood available in Citrus County and beyond.

Quality, freshness, and genuine family values are at the heart of everything we do. Whether you visit our retail store in Floral City, catch us at a regional farmers' market, or grab a bite at our Friday food truck, you're tasting true Gulf-to-table freshness backed by a community that trusts us.
